Popular Diets

Keto Diet

Benefits: Fast fat loss, appetite control.

Cons: Nutrient deficiencies if not planned well.

Sample Meal: Avocado, salmon, spinach salad.

Mediterranean Diet

Benefits: Heart health, longevity.

Cons: Requires fresh ingredients.

Sample Meal: Grilled fish, olive oil, quinoa.

Vegan Diet

Benefits: Ethical, eco-friendly, anti-inflammatory.

Cons: Watch for B12, protein intake.

Sample Meal: Chickpea curry, brown rice.

Paleo Diet

Benefits: High protein, clean eating.

Cons: Restrictive, expensive ingredients.

Sample Meal: Grilled chicken, sweet potato, greens.

Intermittent Fasting

Benefits: Weight control, better metabolism.

Cons: Hard to start, low energy during fasts.

Sample Plan: 16/8 fasting (eating between 12-8 PM).

Training Schedules

Beginner

  • Monday - Full Body (3x12 squats, push-ups, planks)
  • Wednesday - Cardio (20min HIIT)
  • Friday - Full Body (3x15 lunges, rows, crunches)

Intermediate

  • Monday - Upper Body Strength (Bench, Pull-ups)
  • Tuesday - HIIT (Sprints, Burpees)
  • Thursday - Lower Body Strength (Deadlifts, Squats)
  • Saturday - Flexibility (Yoga, Mobility drills)

Advanced

  • Push Day (Chest, Shoulders, Triceps)
  • Pull Day (Back, Biceps)
  • Leg Day (Quads, Hamstrings, Glutes)
  • Conditioning & Core (Battle ropes, Deadbugs)
